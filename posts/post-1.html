Building the First Version

WabsWorld is being rebuilt as a personal web log, project archive, and strange little corner of the internet.

This first version is intentionally simple. No CMS, no framework, no complicated build system. Just HTML, CSS, a few folders, and enough structure to keep writing without breaking the whole site.

The goal is to make a place that can hold projects, screenshots, thoughts, notes, links, experiments, and things that do not fit cleanly on social media.

What this site needs to do

It needs to be easy to update, weird enough to feel personal, and clean enough that somebody else can still read it.
