Making a Scene

By way of house music and bright lights, Coberino is making a scene in Traverse City.

A look into Coberino's budding impact on the local music scene.

Leland raised Cobe Lund has been honing his craft as a DJ by performing for his dogs in the living room, and boatside with friends. With his core support network recognizing his progression and dedication, he has quickly found himself in a position to influence and energize Traverse City's nightlife.

From my perspective.

As one that regularly goes out in Traverse City, I go with the intention of experiencing loud music, joyous people, and dancing. I have a usual route I follow Fridays and Saturdays, seeking this familiar high. Through the city's two staple dance venues I see familiar faces, who spend time at one spot, then moving to the next, at around midnight everyone ends up at SideTraxx, away from bachelorette parties and tourists.

Which brings us to Cobe, an old friend of mine who I see on snapchat posting himself mixing, working out, and throwing together expensive outfits. I noticed that he was finally taking his skill to a venue, posting the flyer for his first show at VI Grill in Suttons Bay. Unfortunately I missed this one, but within the week he surprised me by booking the rooftop of Hotel Indigo in Traverse City for a full 5 hour saturday night set the week after his first show.
